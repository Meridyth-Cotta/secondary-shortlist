# London Secondary Shortlist 2028

A comparison of 27 London secondary schools for Year 7 entry in September 2028.

**Live page:** https://meridyth-cotta.github.io/secondary-shortlist/

Filter by school type across the top, then open any school for the detail behind it.

## What each school record covers

- The admissions mechanism that actually decides a place — distance cut-offs with the measurement method, ability banding, faith scoring, or the entrance test
- Alternative routes in: music, art, languages and sport aptitude places, and whether each bypasses distance or faith
- Registration windows, test dates, results timing and registration fees, each labelled **confirmed**, **projected** or **estimated**
- Fees per term for independent schools, VAT included, with bursary and scholarship provision
- Results, destinations, intake numbers and competition ratios
- Open days, booking method and whether year-round tours exist

## Two accordions at the top

**How the process works** — a briefing on the Pan-London system, supplementary forms, ranking strategy, entrance exam content and visiting, from a Year 5 and Year 6 parents' meeting in September 2026.

**Every open day, in date order** — 58 events across the autumn, colour-coded by school type, with past dates marked.

## Sourcing

Figures come from each school's own determined admissions policy, local authority offer statistics, Department for Education performance data, and Ofsted or Independent Schools Inspectorate reports. Fields that could not be verified from a primary source are marked **not published** rather than estimated. Where two sources disagree, both are reported.

## Important caveat

Admissions arrangements for September 2028 entry are determined by 28 February 2027 and published by 15 March 2027. Everything here is drawn from the 2027-entry cycle and will be superseded. Re-check each school's own policy before relying on it.
